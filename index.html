Research

Responsible model evolution for graph-based AI.

My research addresses the practical and scientific questions that arise after an AI system has been trained: how to remove the influence of designated data, verify that removal transparently, and evaluate model updates under realistic privacy and data-governance requirements.

01

Efficient graph unlearning

Targeted and subgraph-local update strategies that reduce the cost of full retraining while preserving retained-data utility and privacy-oriented behavior.

02

Transparent verification

Explanation-shift, structural, and privacy diagnostics for assessing whether the influence of requested graph entities has been removed.

03

Realistic and auditable evaluation

Natural-language deletion requests, data-quality-triggered forget sets, provenance-aware benchmarks, and reproducible multi-view evaluation.
