Research agenda

Responsible model evolution for graph-based AI.

My research addresses what happens after an AI system has been trained: how designated information can be removed efficiently, how that removal can be verified transparently, and how evaluation can reflect realistic data-governance requests rather than idealized random deletions.

01

Efficient graph unlearning

Request-centred and subgraph-local update strategies that reduce the scope of retraining while preserving retained-data utility and supporting privacy-oriented evaluation.

02

Transparent verification

Explanation-shift, structural, and retrain-aligned diagnostics for determining whether a model has actually removed the influence of designated graph entities.

03

Realistic, auditable evaluation

Natural-language erasure requests, provenance-aware forget sets, and data-quality triggers that connect governance requirements to reproducible graph-learning benchmarks.

Broader interests: explainable AI, privacy-preserving learning, reproducible data science, information retrieval, retrieval-augmented recommendation, and AI for software engineering.
